  <div class="tt-workflow">
    <div class="tt-workflow-intro">
      <span class="tt-kicker">Quick start</span>
      <h2>From download to your first route.</h2>
      <p>Setup is intentionally short. Once your key and dependencies are in place, most collection runs begin with a single choice.</p>
      <div class="tt-paths">
        <div class="tt-path"><strong>Fastest start</strong><p>Target an NPC and choose <em>Farm Target Drops</em>.</p></div>
        <div class="tt-path"><strong>Best for progress</strong><p>Open Card Catalog and multi-select missing cards.</p></div>
        <div class="tt-path"><strong>Hands-off sweep</strong><p>Choose <em>Farm All Missing NPC Cards</em>.</p></div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="tt-steps">
      <article class="tt-step">
        <span class="tt-step__number">1</span>
        <div><h3>Install the plugin</h3><p>Download the ZIP, open its Windows Properties, choose <strong>Unblock</strong> if the option appears, then extract every file into <code>RebornBuddy\Plugins\PandaTripleTriad\</code>. Avoid an extra nested folder.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article class="tt-step">
        <span class="tt-step__number">2</span>
        <div><h3>Enable it in RebornBuddy</h3><p>Open FINAL FANTASY XIV and log in, restart RebornBuddy, open the Plugins tab, and enable <strong>Panda Triple Triad</strong>. Panda Triple Triad is standalone and does not require Panda Farmer WPF.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article class="tt-step">
        <span class="tt-step__number">3</span>
        <div><h3>Activate your key</h3><p>Open the plugin settings, go to <strong>Key</strong>, paste the product key sent to your purchase email, and select <strong>Verify</strong>.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article class="tt-step">
        <span class="tt-step__number">4</span>
        <div><h3>Choose a collection goal</h3><p>Use <strong>Play &amp; Collect</strong> for the NPC in front of you, <strong>NPC Routes</strong> for a broad sweep, or <strong>Card Catalog</strong> to build a route from specific missing cards.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article class="tt-step">
        <span class="tt-step__number">5</span>
        <div><h3>Start and follow the overlay</h3><p>The plugin checks your progress, builds the required battles and travel steps, and starts Order Bot. The compact overlay shows what it is doing and lets you request a gentle stop.</p></div>
      </article>
    </div>

<section class="tt-section">
  <div class="tt-section-heading">
    <span class="tt-kicker">Common questions</span>
    <h2>What to expect.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="tt-faq">
    <details>
      <summary>Will it farm every Triple Triad card automatically?</summary>
      <p>It automates supported NPC drops, duties, MGP purchases, card packs, and selected prerequisite quests. Cards without a supported route remain visible in the planner and are marked as guides so you still know what is missing.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Does it use cards I do not own?</summary>
      <p>No. The local optimizer and community recommendation picker both build from cards learned by the active character.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>What does anonymous solver sharing send?</summary>
      <p>When enabled, it shares game-only match information used to improve deck recommendations. It does not send your character name, account details, product key, or other personal data.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How do I stop a long route safely?</summary>
      <p>Select <strong>Stop Gently</strong> on the gameplay overlay. The button tells you whether the plugin will stop after the current match, duty run, or other active task.</p>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Can I try it before buying?</summary>
      <p>Yes. Join the LlamaMagic Discord and use <code>/trial</code> in the bot-spam channel to request a 72-hour trial.</p>
    </details>
