    Everything in one route

    Collect smarter, from first match to last missing card.

    Panda Triple Triad combines collection planning, travel, deck selection, match play, duties, unlock quests, and Gold Saucer chores in one focused interface.

    01

    Collection-aware Card Planner

    Reads the cards your character has already learned and shows only what is missing. Search by name, card ID, route, or action; filter to automated routes; then select several cards and run them together as one generated profile.

    Quick start

    From download to your first route.

    Setup is intentionally short. Once your key and dependencies are in place, most collection runs begin with a single choice.

    Fastest start

    Target an NPC and choose Farm Target Drops.

    Best for progress

    Open Card Catalog and multi-select missing cards.

    Hands-off sweep

    Choose Farm All Missing NPC Cards.

    1

    Install the plugin

    Download the ZIP, open its Windows Properties, choose Unblock if the option appears, then extract every file into RebornBuddy\Plugins\PandaTripleTriad\. Avoid an extra nested folder.

    2

    Enable it in RebornBuddy

    Open FINAL FANTASY XIV and log in, restart RebornBuddy, open the Plugins tab, and enable Panda Triple Triad. Panda Triple Triad is standalone and does not require Panda Farmer WPF.

    3

    Activate your key

    Open the plugin settings, go to Key, paste the product key sent to your purchase email, and select Verify.

    4

    Choose a collection goal

    Use Play & Collect for the NPC in front of you, NPC Routes for a broad sweep, or Card Catalog to build a route from specific missing cards.

    5

    Start and follow the overlay

    The plugin checks your progress, builds the required battles and travel steps, and starts Order Bot. The compact overlay shows what it is doing and lets you request a gentle stop.

    Before you begin

    Requirements and setup notes.

    Required

    • FINAL FANTASY XIV for Windows
    • RebornBuddy with an active license
    • Lisbeth for route navigation
    • Magitek combat routine for supported duty-card farming
    • A valid Panda Triple Triad key or active trial

    Good to know

    • The plugin updates automatically when RebornBuddy restarts.
    • Card planning is based on the active character’s learned collection.
    • Not every card has an automated route; those entries are marked as guides.
    • Some duties or NPCs still require their normal in-game unlock quests.
    • Anonymous match-result sharing is optional and can be disabled in Settings.
    • The interface is localized for English, Japanese, French, German, Chinese, and Korean clients.
    Tip: Use Log Missing Card Quests in the Card Catalog when you want a clear list of incomplete prerequisites and Garland Tools links.
    Common questions

    What to expect.

    Will it farm every Triple Triad card automatically?

    It automates supported NPC drops, duties, MGP purchases, card packs, and selected prerequisite quests. Cards without a supported route remain visible in the planner and are marked as guides so you still know what is missing.

    Does it use cards I do not own?

    No. The local optimizer and community recommendation picker both build from cards learned by the active character.

    What does anonymous solver sharing send?

    When enabled, it shares game-only match information used to improve deck recommendations. It does not send your character name, account details, product key, or other personal data.

    How do I stop a long route safely?

    Select Stop Gently on the gameplay overlay. The button tells you whether the plugin will stop after the current match, duty run, or other active task.

    Can I try it before buying?

    Yes. Join the LlamaMagic Discord and use /trial in the bot-spam channel to request a 72-hour trial.
